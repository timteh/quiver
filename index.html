How trading works on Robinhood Chain

  1. Get a wallet

    Use a wallet that flap.sh offers under Connect Wallet, such as MetaMask, OKX Wallet, Trust Wallet, Binance Wallet or Bitget Wallet (WalletConnect is also listed). Write your recovery phrase down offline and never share it.

  2. Select Robinhood Chain

    In MetaMask, open Networks and add Robinhood Chain from the list. In other wallets, add it manually with the details below.

  3. Get ETH on Robinhood Chain

    Gas is paid in ETH, and flap.sh trades on Robinhood Chain use ETH. Bridge ETH from Ethereum with the official Arbitrum bridge (deposits usually arrive in about 10 minutes; withdrawals back take about 7 days), or use one of the routes listed in the Robinhood Chain bridging docs.

  4. Open the token on flap.sh

    Open the official flap.sh token page, press Connect Wallet, make sure the network shows Robinhood, choose Buy, enter an ETH amount, and confirm in your wallet. You can lose everything you put in.

    Hold 10,000+ $QVR and earn ETH from every buy and sell: 50% of the tax (after Flap's cut) goes to holders. Tax token: 3% on buys and 3% on sells, fixed at launch (on the bonding curve, flap.sh's 1% curve fee is added on top). Flap takes a protocol cut of the collected tax first (10% of it). Of what remains, 50% is redistributed to wallets holding at least 10,000 tokens, paid in ETH (as WETH); 20% goes to the creator's wallet; 30% is added to liquidity. Amounts depend entirely on trading volume and can be zero. After graduation, collected tax is periodically sold for ETH by the contract. Flap's admin controls the fee contracts and can change where the creator share is sent. Flap lists no audit for Tax Token V3.

  5. Check the contract address

    Before you confirm, check that the address on the flap.sh page and in your wallet matches the one above, character for character. You can also look it up on the explorer.
